# Unclassified covers

Photos with no reliable location or identification yet — **not** part of
`data/covers.geojson` (the schema requires real coordinates; these have
none, not even approximate). Kept here so they aren't lost, until GPS,
memory, or a match against a known design turns up.

Images: `assets/photos/_unclassified/<slug>.webp` + `.thumb.webp` (EXIF
already stripped; the source photos came via WhatsApp, which strips GPS
on its own before we ever saw them).

To promote one to a real entry: add a Feature to `data/personal/mine.json`
(see `_template.json`) with real coordinates, then delete its row below
and its two image files here.
